What this data can and cannot show

  • This is not total European defence procurement. Many large purchases never appear on TED, for example under Article 346 TFEU or in government-to-government deals.
  • Notice counts measure publication activity, not spending. Values are not summed because currencies, frameworks and lot structures are not comparable without further work.
  • A notice can be a correction or a later stage of the same procedure, so notices are not unique purchases.
  • The filter uses CPV groups 353 to 358, so it misses defence purchases coded elsewhere and includes any notice that carries one of these codes.
